Welcome to Heritage Granite

Based in the heart of Sangagiri's stone industry, Heritage Granite has spent over two decades curating the finest natural materials for prestigious projects across India. We believe that every stone tells a story of millennia, and our mission is to help you select the perfect piece for your architectural narrative.

Our showroom offers a tactile experience where you can witness the raw beauty of earth-born materials, from the deepest charcoals to the most ethereal white marbles.
